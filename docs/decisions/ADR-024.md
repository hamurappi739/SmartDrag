# ADR-024 — G3 image behavior is defined independently from the chosen codec

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-31

## Decision

Product-visible behavior for Compress, Convert to WebP, and Remove Metadata is represented as codec-neutral semantics in `MvpImageActionSemantics`.

Exact encoder quality/effort parameters remain G3 benchmark decisions. They must not be embedded in product/UI contracts.

## Why

Changing SkiaSharp/ImageSharp/native codec versions or encoder tuning must not silently redefine what an action means to the user.
